# Archiving and Copyright Tools

**Category:** Technology Domain

## Description

Knowledge of the archiving and copyright tools

## Why it matters

Perservation of our data is important. It prevents data loss, meets legal requirements and increases secuity.

## Target competency

> Can promote archiving and proper use and licensing of intellectual property.

## Progression by component

_Each row is a level a learner is **at**; its activities are what they do to reach the level in the final column._

### 1.0 — General

| Current level | Suggested activities | Reaches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0 - No Competency** | Identify the various Creative Commons licenses. Learn what metadata is. Complete the SIL intellectual property courses. | 1 - Has Knowledge |
| **1 - Has Knowledge** | Learn how to use the licensing components properly in various LT tools. | 2 - With Assistance |
| **2 - With Assistance** | Learn how to use REAP and RAMP for archiving data | 3 - Independent |
| **3 - Independent** | Teach others to use the licensing components in LT tools | 4 - Expert |
| **4 - Expert** | Teach others to use REAP and RAMP | — |

_Level 4 activities aim past the top of the CBC scale (the source spreadsheet's "level 5"), which CBC does not define._
