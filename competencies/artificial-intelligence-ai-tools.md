# Artificial Intelligence (AI) Tools

**Category:** Technology Domain

## Why it matters

AI tools have become foundational to modern language technology work. This competency enables consultants to amplify their productivity, offload routine cognitive work, sharpen decision-making, and accelerate innovation. Just as importantly, it equips consultants to evaluate AI output critically and to apply AI responsibly in contexts where outputs touch Scripture, sensitive minority-language data, and the trust of partner communities.

## Target competency

> Is able to apply general and specialized AI tools effectively, critically, and responsibly in language technology work, and can guide others in their use.

## Progression by component

_Each row is a level a learner is **at**; its activities are what they do to reach the level in the final column._

| **1 - Has Knowledge** | Crafts effective prompts using techniques such as clear context-setting, role assignment, few-shot examples, and structured-output requests. Can compare and choose among major tools for different needs. | 2 - With Assistance |

| **3 - Independent** | Trains and coaches others in effective use of general AI tools, including running workshops, producing reference material, and providing one-on-one support. | 4 - Expert |

### 3.0 — Critical Evaluation and Quality Assurance of AI Output

| Current level | Suggested activities | Reaches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0 - No Competency** | Recognizes that AI outputs can be wrong, biased, or fabricated — and that this risk is heightened for low-resource languages and for theological, cultural, and named-entity content. | 1 - Has Knowledge |
| **1 - Has Knowledge** | Routinely verifies AI-generated content against authoritative sources. Identifies common failure modes (hallucinated citations, mistranslated proper nouns, dropped negations, register drift, doctrinal inaccuracy, fluent-but-wrong output) | 2 - With Assistance |
| **2 - With Assistance** | Designs and applies systematic review processes for AI-assisted work — spot-checking, back-translation comparison, reference-matching, and human-in-the-loop validation appropriate to the stakes of the task. | 3 - Independent |
| **3 - Independent** | Trains others in evaluation methods and helps establish quality-assurance standards for AI use within their team or project. | 4 - Expert |

| Current level | Suggested activities | Reaches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0 - No Competency** | Understands that data submitted to AI tools may be stored, used for model training, or exposed — and that unpublished translations, partner data, and community language data require special care. Aware of relevant SIL and Wycliffe AI guidelines. | 1 - Has Knowledge |
