# Supporting Scripture Pre-publishing Checks
### A Course for Language Technology Consultants

**In production** — this course is being built through the repo's

---

## About This Course

**Audience:** Language Technology Consultants who support translation teams — not the
translators themselves. You are the person a team calls when the pre-publishing checks are
producing results nobody can interpret, or when the typesetter has sent the files back.

**Goal:** Lead a translation team through the pre-publishing **text checks** in Paratext, from a
consultant-checked draft to publication-ready output the typesetter can accept, and advise the
team on the decisions the checks surface.

**Scope:** The text checks — comparing parallel passages, proper names and biblical terms,
numbers/weights/measures, and the final formatting-check pass with its draft-print
verification. The publication *furniture* (illustrations, front and back matter, glossary
linking) is a separate course, not yet built.

**Outcome level:** `3 - Independent` — you run the pass unaided and advise the team, with a
self-check rubric rather than a mentor in the loop.

---

## Relationship to the team workbook

This course is the **consultant-facing** counterpart to
[Scripture Pre-publishing Checks](../scripture-pre-publishing-checks/README.md) — the
multi-chapter workbook (*"Finalizing Your Translation for Publication"*) that a team works
through during the final pre-publishing workshop. That workbook is written for **experienced
translators** doing the work; this course prepares the consultant who **supports** them.

Use them together: the workbook is the handout you give the team; this course is your own
preparation. Both follow [Stage 6 of the Paratext Training Manual](https://manual.paratext.org).
