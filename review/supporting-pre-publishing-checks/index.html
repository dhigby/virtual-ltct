    About This Course¶

    Audience: Language Technology Consultants who support translation teams — not the translators themselves. You are the person a team calls when the pre-publishing checks are producing results nobody can interpret, or when the typesetter has sent the files back.

    Goal: Lead a translation team through the pre-publishing text checks in Paratext, from a consultant-checked draft to publication-ready output the typesetter can accept, and advise the team on the decisions the checks surface.

    Scope: The text checks — comparing parallel passages, proper names and biblical terms, numbers/weights/measures, the final formatting-check pass with its draft-print verification, and the glossary-linking operation and its checks (over-linking vs. first-per-section). The publication furniture — illustrations, front and back matter, and glossary content itself (as opposed to the linking operation) — is a separate course, not yet built.

    Outcome level: 3 - Independent — you run the pass unaided and advise the team. That claim is earned through a mandatory, mentor-reviewed scenario bank, not a self-check rubric: a mentor scores your reasoning on each scenario against the mentor guide's watch-for list, since independence at this level is demonstrated through coaching quality, not a single correct answer.

    Relationship to the team workbook¶

    This course is the consultant-facing counterpart to Scripture Pre-publishing Checks — the multi-chapter workbook ("Finalizing Your Translation for Publication") that a team works through during the final pre-publishing workshop. That workbook is written for experienced translators doing the work; this course prepares the consultant who supports them.

    Use them together: the workbook is the handout you give the team; this course is your own preparation. Both follow Stage 6 of the Paratext Training Manual.
