# Quickstart: verifying identity protection

**Plan**: [plan.md](plan.md) · **Research**: [research.md](research.md)

Amended by the scope review (Doug, 2026-10-05 (scope review)): the checks below test protection for a person who asks, with no organisation minimums, no field locks, no certificate-name field and email kept in staff views.

## Prerequisites

- `MOODLE_URL` and `MOODLE_TOKEN` for the 5.2.3+ test instance, from the environment only.
- **Test accounts only** (constitution III). Name them `ltct-test-*`. Every "real identity", email address and pseudonym is an obviously fake value (`ltct-test-real-1`, `ltct-test-pseudo-1`). No real person's name, email or photo is used.
- **Evidence.** Screenshots, exports and notes that show only `ltct-test-*` accounts may go in the delivering PR (Doug, 2026-10-05 (scope review), change 24). Anything that shows a real person stays in the site team's private store.
- Spec 003 merged, and the open-courses change applied (no organisation groups in courses).
- Two test organisations, A and B. Accounts:
  - learners A1 (to be protected), A2 and B1;
  - A's manager and B's manager;
  - mentor M, from B, assigned to A1 (003), and also enrolled in the course as a learner;
  - course leader T (`editingteacher`), course mentor N (`teacher`) with A1 in N's group `ltct:mentorgroup:<N's id>` in the course, and course mentor N2 (`teacher`) in the same course whose group does not hold A1;
  - a site-team account.
- One open test course with A1, A2, B1 and M enrolled. It has a forum, a workshop, an assignment, a quiz, the 013 badge and certificate, and, once 011 lands, a scheduler with M's slots.
- Spec 009's V7 Android device, signed in as A2, and a second device signed in as M.

## Real users (SC-007)

2–3 real protected users, or people acting for them, compare what others see with what they expected. This is spec 016's own pilot. If three real protected people do not exist yet, each person is asked at their first grant, and the count builds as they come (Doug, 2026-10-05 (scope review), change 25). Their findings are recorded in the site team's private store. **The PR records only the count who confirmed (for example "2 of 3") and the issues found.** It carries no pseudonyms, organisations, locations, screenshots or quotes.
