## Introduction

There are thousands of passages in the New Testament where the same event is described, or where a verse is quoted from the Old or New Testament. These parallel passages need to be compared to make sure they are consistent.

This is a final pre-publishing pass: normally you will already have translated and consultant-checked the text of both books. Here you confirm the parallel passages agree, correct any that don't, and mark them as checked so nothing inconsistent reaches print.

> **INFO** The parallel passages need to be *consistent*, but they do **not** always need to be exactly the same. It is the **meaning** — not necessarily the form — that matters. The Parallel Passages tool displays the passages side by side and highlights the text that is the same.

### What This Chapter Covers

- Opening the Parallel Passages tool and displaying the source texts
- Selecting comparative texts and applying filters
- Comparing passages and interpreting the highlight colours
- Correcting and copying text between passages
- Marking passages as checked (the three approval modes)
- Re-checking passages that have changed since they were marked finished
- Viewing a second and third passage for comparison

### Prerequisites

- You have translated **and** consultant-checked the text of both parallel books.
- You are familiar with the Parallel Passages tool from earlier training.
- You have editing permissions for the books involved.

## Procedure Walkthrough

### Step 1 — Open the Parallel Passages window

1. Navigate to the desired chapter and verse.
2. **≡ Tab**, under **Tools > Parallel passages**.
   - *A window is displayed showing a list and the parallel passages.*

### Step 2 — Display the source texts

1. Click the dropdown arrow beside the source-text options.
2. Choose as appropriate.
   - *The display changes to show the source-language text at the top of the table.*

> **TIP** From Paratext 9.3 on, you can reduce or expand the Greek / Hebrew by clicking the little arrow.

<!-- image: 586542551.png, 406509394.png -->

### Step 3 — Select comparative texts

1. Click the **Comparative texts** button.
2. Click a resource (on the left).
3. Click the right arrow.
4. Repeat as necessary.
5. Click **OK**.

### Step 4 — Apply filters

- Choose the filters as desired (e.g. All references, Current book, Synoptic gospels).

### Step 5 — Compare the passages

- Click a line of references in the top pane.
  - *The texts are displayed in the bottom pane.*

> **INFO** Reading the highlight colours:
> - Text is **grey** (in a project) or **green** (in source texts) when it is exactly the same as in the other passage.
> - Text is **yellow** (in source texts) when it has the same meaning even though it is not exactly the same.
> - If the grey is hard to see, go to the main Paratext **Settings** and change the highlight to **Bright** (this also affects highlights in the text and menus).

> **TIP** If the text is **green** in the Greek, then *your* text should also be the same (but darker green). Normally, if the text differs in the Greek, it should differ in your text too — but not always.

### Step 6 — Correct the text

1. Click the blue **Edit** link.
2. Make the corrections.
3. Click **OK**.

### Step 7 — Copy text between passages

> **TIP** If two passages need to be the same, choose one (normally your later translation) and copy it over the other.

**Copy**
1. Click the blue **Edit** link.
2. Select the text.
3. Copy (Ctrl+C).
4. Click **OK**.

**Paste**
1. Click the blue link for the other text.
2. Select the text.
3. Paste (Ctrl+V).
4. Click **OK**.

> **CAUTION** Be careful when there is more than one verse — you don't want to paste the `\v`.

### Step 8 — Mark the passage as checked

- Click the checkbox beside the reference.
  - *The set of references is approved.*

**Three modes for approving.** Open the window's **menu**; under **View** you can choose:
- **Approve Sets of Parallels** — the default.
- **Approve Passages Individually** — individual checkboxes.
- **Approve by Set or Individually** — switch back and forth between modes.

*To change from set to individually:* open the window menu → **View > Approve by Set or Individually**. Initially a checkbox for the entire set shows in the Status column; hover over the lower checkbox for the set, and a pencil icon appears — click the **pencil** to move the checkboxes to the other mode for that set only.

### Step 9 — Re-check passages that have changed

Identify passages changed since they were marked finished:

1. In the Parallel Passages tool, change the passage filter to **Changed Text**.
2. Click the **ab** icon under the passage reference.
   - *A comparison window opens in Paratext.*
3. Change the text as necessary.
4. Return to the Parallel Passages tool (using the task-bar icons).
5. Click the checkbox to mark it finished again.

<!-- image: 1103066999.png -->

### Step 10 — View another passage for comparison (Quick Reference)

> **INFO** Why not "while you translate"? Good practice is to translate parallel passages *independently* first, so you aren't swayed by an earlier — possibly flawed — rendering. By this final-checking stage the text is already translated, so use these views to **compare and check** consistency, not to translate one passage alongside another.

1. Click in your project.
2. **≡ Tab**, under **Tools > Quick reference** (Ctrl+Q).
   - *A floating window opens with the scroll group set to none.*
3. Change the reference to the other passage.

### Step 11 — See a third passage

1. Open your project a second time (**≡ Paratext > Paratext > Open**, and double-click your project).
2. Change the scroll group from **[A]** to **None**.
3. Change the reference to the other passage.

- [ ] Every relevant set of parallel passages compared
- [ ] Inconsistencies corrected (or documented as intentional differences of meaning)
- [ ] All checked passages marked approved
- [ ] "Changed Text" filter run and any changed passages re-checked
